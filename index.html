About Winners Chapel Ibafo

A Family of Faith, Love, and Dominion

Living Faith Church Ibafo is a vibrant parish of the Living Faith Church Worldwide (also known as Winners Chapel International). We are driven by a burning passion to see lives radically transformed by the power of God's Word.

"The hour has come to liberate the world from all oppressions of the devil through the preaching of the Word of Faith; and I am sending you to undertake this task."
— Bishop David O. Oyedepo (Presiding Bishop)

Under the pastoral leadership of Pastor Babatunde Olaitan, Living Faith Church Ibafo continues to be a lighthouse of spiritual illumination along the Lagos-Ibadan corridor, birthing giants and raising people of purpose.

Undiluted Word: Practical Bible teachings that deliver measurable proofs.
Loving Fellowship: A warm, caring community where nobody is a stranger.
Supernatural Proofs: Constant testimonies of healings, turnarounds, and open doors.

Prayer of Salvation

If you want to accept Jesus Christ as your personal Lord and Saviour, pray this prayer out loud from the depth of your heart:

"Lord Jesus, I come to You today. I acknowledge that I am a sinner and cannot save myself. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose again on the third day for my justification. Today, I confess You with my mouth as my Lord and personal Saviour. Wash away my sins with Your precious blood. Write my name in the Book of Life. Thank You Jesus for saving my soul. I am now born again, a child of God in Jesus' name. Amen!"
